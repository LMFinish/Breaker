One of the rules which allows actors to make use of the zooming colored circle beneath them.

Some actors which have this off by default may still have the circle applied to them through other hardcoded
rules, such as haunted flying objects - which only have the circle applied when they begin flying, but turning
this on for these actors usually will override said rules and make them always use it, even those which never
have the circle applied to them at any point. (A few actually have unused values for this, such as fake doors).
